Ayurix Live Banner

Ayurix Live Banner is a small personal tool that keeps the banner of the Ayurix YouTube channel in sync with its subscriber count. The banner shows a progress bar such as "54 / 100" that updates when the channel gains or loses subscribers.

What it does

  1. On a schedule, a GitHub Actions workflow reads the channel's subscriber count through the YouTube Data API.
  2. If the count changed, it renders a new banner image.
  3. It uploads the image and sets it as the channel banner.

The tool is used only by the owner of the Ayurix channel, on that channel only. It is not offered to other users.
